steps to bake a cake

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease your cake pan.
  2. Mix sugar and butter until creamy, then add eggs and vanilla.
  3. Combine flour, baking powder, and salt in a separate bowl.
  4. Add the dry ingredients to the wet mix, alternating with milk.
  5. Pour the batter into the pan and bake for 30–35 minutes.
  6. Cool the cake, then frost or serve as desired.

Groceries to buy

  • All-purpose flour
  • Sugar
  • Butter
  • Eggs
  • Milk
  • Baking powder
  • Vanilla extract
  • Salt (optional)
